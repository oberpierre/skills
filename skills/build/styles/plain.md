# Plain commit messages

A message is one or two sentences saying what the change does, such as `Link a shipped order's tracking page in its message.` It has no prefix and no suffix: no type, no risk marker and no ticket.

## Corrections

Every correction is a commit of its own, with its own message. History is never rewritten to hide one.

## The gate

The message carries no marker for the gate. The gate still passes before every commit, whatever the style.
